# Enzo’s Concrete LLC: logo and brand kit

Rebuilt on 2026-11-15 from a phone screenshot of the logo (no original source file was available).
The badge was upscaled 4× with the Real-ESRGAN x4plus super-resolution model, then cleaned:
- edge-preserving denoise to remove screenshot and compression mottling
- transparent background re-cut at full resolution, with smoothed anti-aliased edges and no white fringe
- outline snapped to true black
- the blue corrected back to the exact brand blue

## Limits

This is a high-quality raster rebuild, not a vector file. It’s ideal for screens and social media, and
the master prints cleanly up to about 8 inches wide at 300 dpi. For signs, vehicle wraps, embroidery
or large-format print, have a designer redraw the logo as a vector (SVG/AI/EPS) using these files as
the reference.
